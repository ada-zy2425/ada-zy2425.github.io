Multi-view 3D hand reconstruction

Form2Frame

Calibrated multi-view hand meshes from frozen monocular priors

Form2Frame turns synchronized, calibrated views into one metric hand mesh while HaMeR and WiLoR stay frozen. Per-joint view weights suppress occluded views before triangulation. Every monocular mesh is then placed into a shared metric frame by robust Sim(3), and each anatomical region picks its best source mesh.

4.39 mm mesh MPJPE on DexYCB-MV with no alignment of any kind. Vertex error is 26% lower than HaMeR with uniform triangulation on DexYCB-MV, and 30% lower on HanCo.

Two-view geometry

Extreme-View Geometry Recovery (EVGR)

Routing between complementary relative-pose hypotheses

Dense matching, depth-assisted solving and direct camera regression each fail on different image pairs. EVGR keeps all three as complete pose hypotheses and trains a small router that predicts each one's rotation and translation error from geometry alone. It leaves the matching pose only when another candidate is predicted better on both axes.

71.87% AUC@5 on MegaDepth-1500 (71.71% with a second seed), up from 70.25% for RoMa-Ω with PoseLib and above the 71.6% reported by the PoseAgent preprint.

Monocular 3D hand reconstruction

CondDepth

Camera-space hand placement from a single RGB image

In a single image, hand size and depth are confounded. WiLoR's native scale turns out to be a gauge that absorbs pose and projection error, not a physical size. CondDepth therefore keeps WiLoR's hand shape and learns only where the wrist sits along its camera ray.

Camera-space MPJPE of 27.40 mm on DexYCB and 32.39 mm on HO3D v3, below the 30.0 and 33.4 mm reported for ScaleHP. FreiHAND is the current target.

Multimodal learning·CVPR 2026

FUSE-Net

Factorize, Reconstruct, Enhance: A Unified Framework for Multimodal Sentiment Analysis

Zhilu Yang, Mingcheng Li

FUSE-Net predicts sentiment from text, audio and video. It splits each modality into shared, specific and noise factors, uses a variational reconstruction branch to check that those factors still carry the signal, and fuses them with per-sample weights and a learned noise gate.

Reported MAE of 0.688 on CMU-MOSI and 0.527 on CMU-MOSEI.

3D Gaussian Splatting

Robust Sparse 3DGS

Sparse-view reconstruction that survives real camera error

An end-to-end COLMAP / VGGT → gsplat pipeline with held-out evaluation, PLY export and a local WebGL viewer. I traced two failures in sparse-view training, an initialization crash and a large sensitivity to camera rotation, and repaired both with controlled experiments.
