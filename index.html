Research in progress·Multi-view hand reconstruction

MetricMVHand

Measurement-First Multi-View Hand Reconstruction

MetricMVHand combines learned 2D observation correction and relative view weighting with weighted DLT, then couples the reconstructed geometry to a frozen HaMeR prior for metric camera-space hand reconstruction.

DexYCB-MV matched evaluation: 4.731 mm MPJPE and 4.742 mm MPVPE.

Research in progress·Two-view geometry

Fit-Disjoint Verification

Extreme-View Relative Pose

This work separates correspondences used to fit pose hypotheses from those used to verify them, then selects a robust existing hypothesis through geometry-based scoring and pose-medoid consensus.

S7C2 (800 pairs): Success@1° +14.13 pp and AUC@1 +8.08 points, with positive gains reproduced in replication and Full32 settings.

Research in progress·Camera-space recovery

CondDepth

Projective Root Calibration for Camera-Space Hand Recovery

CondDepth calibrates the global UVZ root of a frozen crop-based hand reconstructor using camera intrinsics and crop metadata, recovering metric camera-space placement without changing articulation.

DexYCB S0 camera-space MPJPE: 28.65 mm on the matched primary subset and 30.99 mm on the full population.
