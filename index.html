Research in progress·Multi-view hand reconstruction

Form2Frame

Prior-Preserving Evidence Fusion for Multi-View Hand Reconstruction

Form2Frame places HaMeR and WiLoR hand meshes in a shared metric frame, then fuses complementary candidates across anatomical regions using geometric and visual evidence. A retained baseline mesh provides a fallback.

Evaluated on HanCo, DexYCB-MV, and HO3D-MV. DexYCB-MV: 4.398 mm MPVPE and 4.316 mm mesh MPJPE.

Research in progress·Two-view geometry

Extreme-View Geometry Recovery (EVGR)

Multi-Crop Verification for Relative Pose Estimation

EVGR generates five relative-pose candidates from global and crop-based matches, scores them against shared multi-crop correspondences using geometric consistency, and refines the selected pose using its supporting correspondences.

Evaluated on 30,557 AerialExtreMatch pairs, with a separate A800 reference replay on a 4,096-pair subset.

Research in progress·Geometric calibration

CondDepth

Local-Tangent Ray Calibration for Metric Hand Reconstruction

CondDepth learns camera- and joint-specific corrections in the local tangent plane of predicted camera rays while keeping the vision model frozen. The corrected rays are triangulated to recover metric 3D hand joints.

DexYCB development evaluation with oracle crops and 2,040 labeled calibration views: 20.35 mm median CS-MPJPE, versus 26.95 mm for matched-label readout adaptation.
