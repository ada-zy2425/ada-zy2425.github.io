About Me

I work on 3D computer vision: multi-view geometry, 3D reconstruction and camera pose estimation. I am interested in recovering reliable metric 3D geometry when views, models or detectors disagree or fail.

Selected Research

AlignHand: Multi-View Hand Reconstruction from a Frozen Monocular Prior

Zhilu Yang, Kun Yang, Shiyan Wang, Anqi Xu, Xinran Huang, Yizheng Jiao, Huan Yang, Rolandos Alexandros Potamias

Preprint, 2026

Turns conflicting per-view predictions of a frozen monocular hand model into one metric hand mesh, with 30% lower vertex error than a matched geometric baseline.

EviPose: Evidence-Driven Two-View Relative Pose Estimation

Ongoing research

Complementary geometric observations, task-aligned pose recovery and evidence-based fallback, evaluated on MegaDepth-1500, ScanNet-1500 and RUBIK.

PlaceHand: Monocular 3D Hand Reconstruction and Camera-Space Placement

Ongoing research

Places the hand in metric camera space from one RGB image, with detection-aware recovery when the hand detector fails.

Robust Sparse 3DGS: Sparse-View Reconstruction under Camera Perturbation

A COLMAP / VGGT → gsplat pipeline with held-out evaluation and a WebGL viewer; rotation-only camera correction recovers over 96% of injected rotation error.
